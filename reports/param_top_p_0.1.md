# Business Impact Assessment Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This report analyzes recent supply chain disruptions affecting TransGlobal Manufacturing, focusing on their root causes, impacts on suppliers, inventory, production, logistics, and financial implications. Based on the analysis, tailored mitigation strategies are recommended to enhance resilience and ensure business continuity.

## 2. Identified Disruptions
- **Natural Disasters**: Tsunami, flooding, earthquake, typhoon.
- **Supplier Bankruptcy**: Cash-flow crises affecting Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers.
- **Quality Failures**: Defective batches leading to significant production downtime.
- **Transport Disruptions**: Rail network failures and port congestion.
- **Labor Strikes**: Protests affecting operations across various regions.
- **Cyber Attacks**: Network outages impacting IT infrastructure.
- **Customs Delays**: Documentation errors causing shipment delays.

## 3. Root Causes
- **Natural Disasters**: Environmental factors leading to physical damage and operational delays.
- **Financial Instability**: Supplier bankruptcies due to cash-flow issues.
- **Quality Control Failures**: Defective materials impacting production quality.
- **Transport Infrastructure Issues**: Rail network failures and port congestion causing delays.
- **Labor Relations**: Protests and strikes due to working conditions.
- **Cybersecurity Vulnerabilities**: Breaches affecting operational capabilities.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Customs delays due to documentation errors.

## 4. Supplier Impact
- **Tier 1 Suppliers**: Directly affected by natural disasters and quality failures, leading to significant production downtime.
- **Tier 2 Suppliers**: Vulnerable to financial instability and transport disruptions, impacting their ability to deliver materials on time.
- **Tier 3 Suppliers**: Less critical but still impacted by broader supply chain issues, particularly in transport and quality.

## 5. Inventory Impact
- **Decreased Inventory Levels**: Significant drops in Days of Inventory on Hand (DOH) observed post-disruption.
- **Increased Lead Times**: Extended lead times due to disruptions, affecting replenishment cycles.
- **Emergency Allocations**: Necessity for emergency inventory allocations to mitigate immediate impacts.

## 6. Production Risk
- **Downtime**: High production downtime hours reported, particularly from quality failures and transport disruptions.
- **Affected SKUs**: A large number of SKUs impacted, leading to potential revenue loss and customer dissatisfaction.

## 7. Logistics Risk
- **Increased Costs**: Notable increases in logistics costs due to expedited shipping and alternative transport modes.
- **Transport Delays**: Significant delays in shipments affecting overall supply chain efficiency.

## 8. Financial Implications
- **Revenue at Risk**: Substantial revenue at risk due to disruptions, with some events leading to millions in potential losses.
- **Financial Impact**: High financial impacts from expedite costs and increased logistics expenses.

## 9. Recommended Mitigation Strategies
### Natural Disasters
- **Strategy**: Enhance disaster recovery plans and invest in resilient infrastructure.
- **Rationale**: Proactive measures can minimize downtime and expedite recovery.

### Supplier Bankruptcy
- **Strategy**: Diversify supplier base and establish financial health monitoring.
- **Rationale**: Reduces dependency on single suppliers and mitigates financial risks.

### Quality Failures
- **Strategy**: Implement stricter quality control measures and 100% inspections.
