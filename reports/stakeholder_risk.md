# Risk Management Team Report

# Business Impact Assessment Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This report provides a comprehensive analysis of recent supply chain disruptions faced by TransGlobal Manufacturing, outlining their impacts, root causes, and potential mitigation strategies. The disruptions predominantly stem from natural disasters, quality failures, transport issues, and labor strikes. This assessment aims to identify the most appropriate strategies to mitigate risks and ensure business continuity.

## 2. Identified Disruptions
- Natural Disasters: Tsunami, flooding, earthquake, and typhoon events affecting operations in Asia and North America.
- Quality Failures: Defective materials causing significant production downtime in South America and Europe.
- Transport Disruptions: Rail network failures and port congestion impacting multiple facilities.
- Labor Strikes: Protests affecting operations in North America and South America.

## 3. Root Causes
- **Natural Disasters**: Environmental factors such as tsunamis and floods causing facility damage and transport delays.
- **Quality Failures**: Inadequate supplier quality control leading to defective materials.
- **Transport Issues**: Infrastructure challenges including rail failures and port congestion.
- **Labor Strikes**: Employee grievances regarding working conditions resulting in operational halts.

## 4. Supplier Impact
- Significant impact on Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers, particularly those providing critical materials like aluminum and semiconductors.
- Supplier bankruptcies leading to increased procurement challenges and a need for emergency sourcing.
- Quality issues from suppliers causing extensive downtimes and increased costs.

## 5. Inventory Impact
- Fluctuations in inventory days on hand (DOH) due to disruptions, impacting the ability to meet production demands.
- Some events resulted in a drop in inventory levels, which may jeopardize the ability to fulfill orders.

## 6. Production Risk
- High production downtime hours reported, particularly in severe events such as the labor strikes and quality failures.
- Affected SKUs range from 8 to 546, indicating varying degrees of impact across different product lines.

## 7. Logistics Risk
- Increased logistics costs due to disrupted transport modes and need for expedited shipping.
- Significant shipment delays reported, affecting timely delivery and increasing the risk of lost sales.

## 8. Financial Implications
- Total revenue at risk ranges significantly, with some events jeopardizing over $5 million in potential revenue.
- Financial impacts from disruptions are substantial, with some events resulting in over $4 million in losses.

## 9. Recommended Mitigation Strategies
### Major Disruption Events
1. **Natural Disasters (EVT-001, EVT-003, EVT-006, EVT-013)**
   - **Mitigation Strategy**: Implement robust disaster recovery plans that include alternative sourcing and inventory allocation.
   - **Recommendation**: Emergency inventory allocation for critical components to minimize production downtime.

2. **Quality Failures (EVT-004, EVT-010, EVT-011)**
   - **Mitigation Strategy**: Enhance supplier quality assurance processes, including 100% inspection of critical materials.
   - **Recommendation**: Establish strategic partnerships with multiple suppliers to diversify risk and reduce dependency.

3. **Transport Disruptions (EVT-005, EVT-012, EVT-018, EVT-023)**
   - **Mitigation Strategy**: Utilize alternate carriers and consolidate shipments to improve delivery reliability.
   - **Recommendation**: Invest in logistics technology to optimize routing and tracking of shipments.

4. **Labor Strikes (EVT-007, EVT-015, EVT-024, EVT-025)**
   - **Mitigation Strategy**: Foster better labor relations and implement contingency workforce plans.
   - **Recommendation**: Engage with employees to address grievances proactively and minimize strike risk.

## 10. Business Continuity Assessment
- Overall business continuity is currently assessed as stable for most events, with ongoing monitoring required for high-impact disruptions.
- Critical disruptions, particularly related to quality failures and severe transport issues, pose significant risks to operations and require immediate action to ensure continuity.
- A proactive approach to risk management, including regular reviews and updates to contingency plans, is essential to navigate future disruptions effectively.

In conclusion, the findings indicate a need for enhanced resilience in supply chain operations through strategic mitigation efforts, improved supplier relationships, and a focus on business continuity planning.
