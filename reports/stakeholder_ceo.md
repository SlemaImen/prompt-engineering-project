# Business Impact Assessment Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This report analyzes a series of supply chain disruptions that occurred between January and March 2026, assessing their impacts on operations, inventory, logistics, and financial performance. The report identifies key disruptions, evaluates root causes, and recommends mitigation strategies to enhance resilience and ensure business continuity.

## 2. Identified Disruptions
- **Natural Disasters**: Tsunami and flooding in South Korea, earthquake in Canada, typhoon in UAE.
- **Supplier Bankruptcy**: Two events in Thailand and Brazil.
- **Quality Failures**: Multiple incidents affecting various materials, notably plastics and steel.
- **Transport Disruptions**: Rail network failures and port congestion in multiple regions.
- **Labor Strikes**: Protests affecting operations in several countries.

## 3. Root Causes
- **Natural Disasters**: Environmental factors leading to significant operational disruptions.
- **Supplier Bankruptcy**: Financial instability of suppliers due to cash-flow crises.
- **Quality Failures**: Defective batches and contamination issues.
- **Transport Disruptions**: Infrastructure failures and port congestion.
- **Labor Strikes**: Employee dissatisfaction related to working conditions.

## 4. Supplier Impact
- **Tier 1 Suppliers**: Most disruptions involved Tier 1 suppliers, indicating a critical reliance on primary suppliers for essential materials.
- **Tier 2 and 3 Suppliers**: Bankruptcy and quality issues in lower-tier suppliers also contributed to significant operational risks.

## 5. Inventory Impact
- **Inventory Levels**: Disruptions led to decreased inventory days on hand (DOH) across various materials, impacting the ability to meet production demands.
- **Emergency Allocations**: Some events required emergency inventory allocations to mitigate immediate impacts.

## 6. Production Risk
- **Downtime**: Significant production downtime recorded, particularly during quality failures and natural disasters, affecting overall output.
- **Affected SKUs**: A large number of SKUs were impacted, particularly during high-severity events.
