# Business Impact Assessment Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This report analyzes recent supply chain disruptions affecting TransGlobal Manufacturing, focusing on their root causes, impacts on suppliers, inventory, production, logistics, and financial implications. Based on the analysis, tailored mitigation strategies are recommended to enhance resilience and ensure business continuity.

## 2. Identified Disruptions
- **Natural Disasters**: Tsunami, flooding, earthquake, typhoon.
- **Supplier Bankruptcy**: Cash-flow crises affecting Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers.
- **Quality Failures**: Defective batches leading to significant production downtime.
- **Transport Disruptions**: Rail network failures and port congestion.
- **Labor Strikes**: Protests affecting operations across various regions.
- **Cyber Attacks**: Network outages impacting IT infrastructure.
- **Customs Delays**: Documentation errors causing shipment delays.

## 3. Root Causes
- **Natural Disasters**: Environmental factors leading to physical damage and operational delays.
- **Supplier Bankruptcy**: Financial instability within suppliers.
- **Quality Failures**: Inadequate quality control measures.
- **Transport Disruptions**: Infrastructure failures and logistical bottlenecks.
- **Labor Strikes**: Employee dissatisfaction regarding working conditions.
- **Cyber Attacks**: Vulnerabilities in IT systems.
- **Customs Delays**: Administrative inefficiencies.

## 4. Supplier Impact
- **Tier 1 Suppliers**: Directly affected by natural disasters and quality failures, leading to significant production downtime.
- **Tier 2 Suppliers**: Vulnerable to bankruptcy and transport disruptions, impacting material availability.
- **Tier 3 Suppliers**: Less critical but still contribute to overall supply chain risk.
