# Business Impact Assessment Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This report analyzes recent supply chain disruptions affecting TransGlobal Manufacturing, focusing on their impact on operations, financials, and business continuity. The assessment identifies key disruptions, evaluates root causes, and recommends mitigation strategies to enhance resilience and minimize future risks.

## 2. Identified Disruptions
- **Natural Disasters**: Events in South Korea (tsunami and flooding), Canada (earthquake), and UAE (typhoon).
- **Supplier Bankruptcy**: Incidents in Thailand and Brazil.
- **Quality Failures**: Multiple occurrences across South America, Europe, and North America.
- **Transport Disruptions**: Rail network failures in Canada and Brazil, and port congestion in Morocco and the UK.
- **Labor Strikes**: Protests in Poland, China, and South America.
- **Cyber Attacks**: Incidents in Brazil and the Netherlands.
- **Customs Delays**: Documentation errors in Thailand.

## 3. Root Causes
- **Natural Disasters**: Environmental factors leading to significant operational disruptions.
- **Supplier Bankruptcy**: Financial instability of suppliers affecting material availability.
- **Quality Failures**: Defective materials leading to production halts.
- **Transport Disruptions**: Infrastructure failures causing delays in logistics.
- **Labor Strikes**: Employee dissatisfaction impacting production capabilities.
- **Cyber Attacks**: Security breaches affecting operational integrity.
- **Customs Delays**: Administrative errors leading to shipment holdups.

## 4. Supplier Impact
- **Tier 1 Suppliers**: Most critical disruptions stem from Tier 1 suppliers, particularly in natural disasters and quality failures.
- **Tier 2 and 3 Suppliers**: Bankruptcy and transport issues predominantly affect Tier 2 and Tier 3 suppliers, leading to increased risk in material sourcing.

## 5. Inventory Impact
- Significant reductions in Days of Inventory on Hand (DOH) observed post-disruption, indicating potential stockouts.
- Affected SKUs range from 8 to 546, with critical inventory shortages impacting production lines.

## 6. Production Risk
- Production downtime varies significantly, with some events causing over 448 hours of downtime.
- High affected SKU counts indicate widespread production risks across multiple facilities.
