# Business Impact Assessment Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This report analyzes a series of supply chain disruptions affecting TransGlobal Manufacturing from January to March 2026. A range of events, including natural disasters, supplier bankruptcies, quality failures, transport disruptions, and labor strikes, has been documented. The assessment focuses on identifying root causes, impacts on suppliers and inventory, production and logistics risks, financial implications, and providing actionable recommendations for mitigation strategies.

## 2. Identified Disruptions
- A total of 25 disruption events recorded.
- Key disruption types include:
  - Natural Disasters (Tsunami, Flooding, Earthquake, Typhoon)
  - Supplier Bankruptcies
  - Quality Failures (Defective Batches, Contamination)
  - Transport Disruptions (Rail Network Failures, Port Congestion)
  - Labor Strikes
  - Cyber Attacks
  - Customs Delays

## 3. Root Causes
- **Natural Disasters:** Environmental events such as tsunamis, flooding, earthquakes, and typhoons.
- **Supplier Bankruptcy:** Cash-flow crises impacting Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers.
- **Quality Failures:** Defective materials and contamination in product batches.
- **Transport Disruptions:** Failures in transport networks, leading to delays.
- **Labor Strikes:** Protests concerning working conditions affecting production.
- **Cyber Attacks:** Breaches in IT systems impacting operations.

## 4. Supplier Impact
- **Tier 1 Suppliers:** Most severely impacted, especially during quality failures and natural disasters.
- **Tier 2 and Tier 3 Suppliers:** More frequent bankruptcies and labor strikes noted, indicating vulnerabilities in lower-tier suppliers.
- **Overall:** Disruptions resulted in decreased reliability and increased scrutiny on supplier performance.

## 5. Inventory Impact
- Significant fluctuations in Days on Hand (Doh) noted, with reductions leading to constraints on production capabilities.
- Critical inventory depletion observed, especially during high-severity events, risking further production delays and increased expedite costs.

## 6. Production Risk
- **Downtime:** Significant production downtime recorded, up to 448 hours in one instance (Event 10).
- **Affected SKUs:** Over 500 SKUs impacted across various events, necessitating urgent remediation efforts to minimize delays.

## 7. Logistics Risk
- Increased logistics costs due to shipment delays and modal changes (e.g., shifting to air freight).
- Port congestion has led to severe delays, impacting multiple facilities and regions, with a notable increase in logistics costs by up to 77%.

## 8. Financial Implications
- Total revenue at risk amounts to approximately $10 million across all events.
- Financial impacts from disruptions can reach over $4 million in single instances, indicating a significant vulnerability to financial loss.

## 9. Recommended Mitigation Strategies
### Major Disruption Events and Mitigation Recommendations
1. **Natural Disasters (e.g., EVT-003, EVT-006, EVT-013):**
   - **Mitigation Strategy:** Enhance disaster recovery plans with emphasis on rapid response teams and emergency inventory allocation.
   - **Recommendation:** Invest in predictive analytics for disaster risk assessment and establish buffer stock in at-risk areas.
