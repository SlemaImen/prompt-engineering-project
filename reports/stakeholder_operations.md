# Business Impact Assessment Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This report analyzes recent supply chain disruptions affecting TransGlobal Manufacturing, focusing on events from January to March 2026. Key disruptions include natural disasters, quality failures, transport disruptions, and labor strikes. The analysis highlights the root causes, impacts on suppliers, inventory, production, logistics, and financial implications, culminating in actionable mitigation recommendations to enhance business continuity.

## 2. Identified Disruptions
- **Natural Disasters**: Events EVT-001, EVT-003, EVT-006, EVT-013
- **Quality Failures**: Events EVT-004, EVT-010, EVT-011, EVT-008, EVT-009, EVT-021
- **Transport Disruptions**: Events EVT-005, EVT-012, EVT-017, EVT-018, EVT-023
- **Supplier Bankruptcies**: Events EVT-002, EVT-022
- **Labor Strikes**: Events EVT-007, EVT-015, EVT-024, EVT-025
- **Cyber Attacks**: Events EVT-016, EVT-019
- **Customs Delays**: Event EVT-020
- **Port Congestion**: Events EVT-014, EVT-021

## 3. Root Causes
- **Natural Disasters**: Tsunami, flooding, earthquake, typhoon
- **Quality Failures**: Defective batches, material substandard, contamination
- **Transport Disruptions**: Fuel shortages, rail network failures, container backlogs
- **Supplier Bankruptcies**: Cash-flow crises
- **Labor Strikes**: Working-conditions protests
- **Cyber Attacks**: System breaches, network outages
- **Customs Delays**: Documentation errors
- **Port Congestion**: Container backlog

## 4. Supplier Impact
- **Tier 1 Suppliers**: Significant impact due to reliance on critical materials (e.g., aluminum, semiconductors).
- **Tier 2 and 3 Suppliers**: Increased vulnerability due to bankruptcy and cash-flow issues affecting procurement.

## 5. Inventory Impact
- Inventory Days of Supply (Doh) decreased in several events, indicating strained inventory levels.
- Affected SKUs range from 8 to 546, emphasizing the breadth of impact across product lines.

## 6. Production Risk
- Production downtime hours varied significantly, with some events causing over 400 hours of downtime.
- High OTD (On-Time Delivery) drop percentages, particularly in quality failure events, highlight production risks.

## 7. Logistics Risk
- Increased logistics costs and shipment delays were prevalent, particularly in transport disruption and quality failure events.
- The need for expedited shipping options resulted in significant additional costs.

## 8. Financial Implications
- Revenue at risk ranged from USD 23,478 to USD 5,364,169 across events, with financial impacts exceeding USD 4 million in critical cases.
- The cumulative financial impact necessitates immediate attention to risk mitigation strategies.

## 9. Recommended Mitigation Strategies
1. **Natural Disasters**:
   - **Deploy Disaster Recovery Plans**: Ensure robust disaster recovery plans are in place and regularly tested (EVT-001, EVT-003).
   - **Emergency Inventory Allocation**: Allocate emergency stock for critical materials in disaster-prone regions (EVT-003, EVT-013).

2. **Quality Failures**:
   - **Implement 100% Inspection**: For critical components, enforce stringent quality checks before acceptance (EVT-010, EVT-011).
   - **Supplier Audits**: Conduct regular audits of suppliers to ensure compliance with quality standards (EVT-004, EVT-009).

3. **Transport Disruptions**:
   - **Diversify Transport Modes**: Use multiple transport options to mitigate risks associated with rail or road failures (EVT-005, EVT-012).
   - **Establish Contingency Plans**: Develop plans for alternate carriers and routes to address transport disruptions proactively (EVT-018, EVT-023).
